How to play
  • Drag a sticker to turn the layer under it. Inner layers turn too, so you can reach every slice of a big cube.
  • Drag the empty mat to look around the cube.
  • Keys U D L R F B turn the outer layers; hold Shift to turn counter-clockwise. Type a number first for an inner layer: 2 then R turns the second layer from the right (2R).
  • Keys follow your view: F is the side facing you.
  • Ctrl+Z or Backspace undoes your last turn. An undo still counts as a move.
  • Give up plays every turn backwards to the solved cube. The attempt counts as DNF (did not finish).
  • The Learn tab has a step-by-step method for each size. The Solver and AI players tabs let a computer play.

Scramble

To copy it onto a real cube, hold it white on top and green in front.

How to read the moves

      The solver reads only the sticker colours. It never sees your scramble or your turns. It moves three pieces at a time, the way blindfold solvers work, so it uses far more turns than a person would.

      It starts slowly, at 2 turns a second. The three pieces it is moving light up in pink. Use the bar on the cube to pause, step, change speed or finish.

        Let an AI play your cube move by move. It sees the colours and the move rules, nothing else. The game scores how many stickers match the main colour of their side.
