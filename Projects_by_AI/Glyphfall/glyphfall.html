Shading

Each spot's brightness picks one of 70 shades made of letters, numbers and symbols, from $ @ B % 8 & W M for the brightest to ^ " ~ ! ; _ : for the darkest (on Paper the other way round).

Shape

Where a spot holds an edge, the character whose own shape lines up with it wins, from all 95 on your keyboard: / \ | _ ( ) [ ] { } < > L J 7 V

Colour

Only the characters are drawn, nothing behind them: each one takes the colour of your picture at its spot, just as bright as it needs to be (on Paper: dark ink on plain paper).
