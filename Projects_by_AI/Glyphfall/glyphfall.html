MAKING THE CHARACTERS…

Shading

The image is cut into cells. Each cell's brightness picks a character by how much ink it has: . : - = + * # % @

Edges

Where a cell holds a real edge, a character whose shape lines up with it wins instead: / \ | _ ( ) < > ^ v

Colour

Each character sits on a dim version of the cell's colour and makes up the rest, so from a step back it matches your picture.
