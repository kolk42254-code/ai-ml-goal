Shading

Each spot's brightness picks one of 70 shades made of letters, numbers and symbols, from $ @ B % 8 & W M for the brightest to ; : , " ^ ` ' . for the darkest.

Shape

Where a spot holds an edge, the character whose own shape lines up with it wins, from all 95 on your keyboard: / \ | _ ( ) [ ] { } < > L J 7 V

Colour

Each character is coloured, and sits on a dim version of the same colour, so that every spot shows exactly the colour of your picture there.
